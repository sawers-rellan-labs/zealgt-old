## 7. Open points

1. **Trimmomatic `-trimlog`** is hard-coded in the nf-core module and is emitted as `trim_log`. Uncompressed, it is about 282 GB per 1A-size library, 36 % of `work/`, and it is used by nothing downstream. Proposal: a module patch that drops `-trimlog` (or writes it to /dev/null), which brings `work/` to ≈ 0.45 TB. This needs the user's decision because it changes the TRIMMOMATIC hash.
2. Position-1 C/T bias split by sample after the crop (§3). It is not a fixed skip base; check whether it is barcode-linked.
3. DEMUX's `zg_resources: WARNING … ZG_JAVA_MEM_MB unset` at 2 GB. It is harmless; the helper could skip the warning for non-JVM modules.
4. The positional-argument `true` WARN in every run (§2).
5. Cleanup needs the user's consent:
   - stub `work/` for gate0_* (~11 MB, 905 files)
   - gate1_* work (428 MB / 927 files)
   - `gate1_import/tmp` (2.8 GB / 34 files, OOM leftover)
   - `cachecheck_fastqc/` (work + three stores on /share)
   - the Gate 1 stores `ZEAL/store/subsample_1000000` and `ZEAL/store_gate1` (small)
6. Picard is single-threaded and has a ~6.5 min floor even on an empty CRAM. With about 130 BC1 samples that is ≥ 15 CPU-h of floor alone. That is acceptable, but `--USE_FAST_ALGORITHM true` (or the `INTERVALS` of one chromosome) is an option if QC time matters.
7. TRIMMOMATIC uses about 3 of 8 cores. 4 cpus would free allocation without slowing it, since it is I/O-bound. Measure at Gate 2 before changing.
8. Earlier handovers' open items are unchanged: `workflows/zealgt.nf`, M14, step 8.
